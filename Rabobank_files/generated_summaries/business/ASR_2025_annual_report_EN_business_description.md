- ASR Nederland N.V. (“a.s.r.”) is the Dutch parent of the a.s.r. group, listed on Euronext Amsterdam/AEX and based in Utrecht. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 13] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 414]  
- The group operates almost exclusively in the Netherlands and reports five segments: Non-life, Life, Asset Management, Distribution and Services, and Holding and Other. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 13-15] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 423]  
- a.s.r. serves private individuals, self-employed professionals and businesses with insurance, pensions, mortgages, distribution and services, and third-party asset management. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 13, 18, 80, 97, 99]  
- In 2025, a.s.r. reported 5.2 million customers, 8,689 full-time equivalents (FTE), assets of €142.2 billion, an operating result of €1,637 million, a Solvency II ratio of 218%, and premiums and defined contribution (DC) inflow of €13,375 million. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 6-7, 49, 65-66]  
- Aegon Ltd. held approximately 23.60% to 24% of the shares, after share repurchases in September 2025 reduced its holding from 29.96% to approximately 24%. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 57-58] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 363]  

### Business model and operations

a.s.r. describes its activities as insurance and financial services, including insurance contracts, investment contracts, investment property, mortgages, distribution and intermediary services, and asset management. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 453-454] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 133, 135, 138-140, 159, 161] Intermediaries advise clients, help them purchase policies, and assist with claims. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 453]  

Its businesses include Asset Management, Real Estate, Mortgages, property and casualty (P&C), Health, pensions administration (TPM), Individual life and Funeral, and HumanTotalCare. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 258-259, 261] The company also reports product lines described as Insurance & Intermediary, Asset Management, and Business Support. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 133, 135]  

a.s.r. is the parent of a group of operating and holding companies and consolidates controlled subsidiaries. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 272] It disclosed non-wholly-owned participations in Robidus Groep B.V. and Robidus Risk Consulting B.V. at 95%, and BSB Assurantiën B.V. at 85%. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 285, 291] On 1 October 2025, a.s.r. acquired the remaining 55% of HumanTouch for €108 million; this transaction generated goodwill of €115 million, a remeasurement gain of €27 million, and a fourth-quarter contribution of €60 million fee income and €4 million net result. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 300-301]  

Reported 2025 financial metrics include an operating result of €1,637 million. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 295-296] Reported net result figures are inconsistent across the provided context, with €565 million, €549 million, and €476 million each stated elsewhere, while basic earnings per share (EPS) was reported at €2.30. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 263] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 411-415] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 314] Reported revenue figures are also inconsistent, with insurance contract revenue stated as €10,324 million in one location and elsewhere as €4,700 million, while another disclosure states total revenue of 19,797. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 261-263] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 194-195, 197] Reported employee figures are inconsistent, with 8,689 internal FTE and elsewhere 9,718 employees, the latter noted as up from 7,998 due to the HumanTotalCare acquisition. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 263] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 209]  

### Markets and strategy

a.s.r. states that it aims, through financial services and its role as a responsible investor, to improve customers’ financial situation and contribute to a sustainable society. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 453-454] Its key stakeholders are customers, employees, shareholders, and society. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 453-454]  

The business is concentrated in the Netherlands, where it serves retail, self-employed, and business clients across insurance, pensions, mortgages, and asset management. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 13, 18, 80, 97, 99] This domestic focus is reflected in management’s description of a.s.r. as operating almost exclusively in the Netherlands. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 13-15]  

In 2025, the company reported that the integration of a.s.r. and Aegon Nederland (Aegon NL) achieved all planned milestones and was fully on track for cost synergies. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 106-107] However, completion timing for the Aegon integration is inconsistent in the provided context, being stated as end-2025 in one place and mid- or end-2026 in another. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 8-9] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 11]  

Capital strength appears important to the group’s strategy and resilience, with the Solvency II ratio rising to 218% in 2025 from 198% in 2024; the ratio was reported as 220% excluding financial institutions, with own funds of €13,007 million and a Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR) of €5,966 million. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 118] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 404-406]  

The company also disclosed European Union (EU) Taxonomy alignment of 11% of turnover and 11% of capital expenditure (CapEx) in 2025, versus 6% and 6% in 2024, mainly from real estate and mortgages. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 193] Reported financed-emissions figures for 2025 are inconsistent in the provided context, with 5,960,069 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e) stated in one place and 17,044,235 tCO2e on €83,288 million of assets under management (AuM) stated elsewhere. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, p. 158] [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 164-165]  

### Principal disclosed risks and responses

a.s.r. identified key risks including cyber risk, artificial intelligence (AI), geopolitical and economic instability, climate risk, regulation, interest rates, mortgage spread risk, inflation, and costs. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 107-108, 117, 119] These risks are relevant to the group’s insurance, mortgage, investment, and operational activities in the Netherlands. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 13-18, 97, 99]  

The company reported responses including scenario analysis, portfolio rebalancing, reinsurance, business continuity management (BCM), a “Doomsday” backup environment, and cryptography updates with key software-as-a-service (SaaS) partners. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 107-108, 117, 119] These measures indicate a focus on maintaining operational resilience, managing investment and underwriting exposures, and strengthening information security in support of its customer-facing insurance and financial-services activities. [2025-annual-report-asr.pdf, pp. 107-108, 117, 119]
