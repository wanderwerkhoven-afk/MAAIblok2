### Business model and operations

VDL Groep supplies components, semi-finished products, and end products, and operates on three continents, with European Union (EU) countries as its main market. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 43] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 56] Its activities include contract manufacturing, build-to-spec production, and original equipment manufacturer (OEM) own products. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 32]

The report describes a broad industrial portfolio that includes turnkey mechatronic systems, subsystem and module integration, technology development for OEMs and high-tech capital goods, electronics, waste systems, packaging machinery, battery assembly, contract manufacturing, and vehicle manufacturing and services. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 69] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 72] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 75] It also names own products including emission-free buses, battery energy storage systems (BESS), hydrogen generators, charging infrastructure, and parcel lockers. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 32]

Within the group structure, named entities include VDL Nederland B.V., VDL International B.V., VDL Nederland Beheer B.V., VDL Bus Group Holding B.V., VDL Car Beheer B.V., and VDL Defentec Beheer B.V. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 64] The group also disclosed the 2025 acquisition of all shares in Crux Agribotics Holding B.V. for an initial agreed price of EUR 3.7 million. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 56]

Selected operating developments illustrate the breadth of the portfolio. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 16] VDL Special Vehicles integrated its Venlo and Eindhoven activities in 2025, obtained ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification, and focuses on vehicle conversions, police vehicles, midibuses, heavy-vehicle electrification, and mobility contract manufacturing. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 16] That business completed the last Mercedes B-Class police vehicles, reported cumulative Mercedes deliveries of more than 1,500 vehicles, and stated that the BMW X1 will succeed that model. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 16]

At segment level, VDL Energy reported revenue of EUR 91 million in 2025 versus EUR 105 million in 2024, while VDL Infratech reported revenue of EUR 152 million versus EUR 144 million, and Foodtech revenue rose to EUR 229 million from EUR 191 million. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 20-21] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 22-23] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 24]

The workforce decreased by 6% to 13,351 from 14,241, with 9,113 employees in the Netherlands, 1,122 in Belgium, 1,856 in other Europe, and 1,260 worldwide outside those areas. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 34] The group also reported that 54% of revenue or products were outside the Netherlands. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 5]

### Markets and strategy

VDL states that its main market is in EU countries, while deliveries reached 115 countries and activities spanned 22 countries in 2025. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 56] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 4] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 10] The group’s entity list shows operations across the Netherlands, France, Belgium, Germany, Poland, Turkey, Singapore, Suzhou, Switzerland, Vietnam, and the United States. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 69] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 70] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 73]

Management describes the strategic focus as long-term continuity, strong finances, controlled growth, competitiveness, and innovation, supported by automation, robotization, and digitalization. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 30] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 43, 45] Sustainability is embedded through a 2025-2027 environmental, social and governance (ESG) strategy based on ten material topics, with reporting-compliance targeted for 2027. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 30, 34]

Investment levels indicate continued development spending despite weaker revenue. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 28] In 2025, VDL invested EUR 120 million in fixed assets and EUR 170 million in research and development (R&D), and expected around EUR 250 million in 2026. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 28] Reported 2025 operating cash flow was EUR 289.624 million and investing cash flow was negative EUR 126.535 million. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 55]

The annual report links weaker 2025 performance mainly to delayed recovery in semiconductors and reduced assembly revenue from the wind-down of VDL Nedcar. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 6] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 51-52] It also states that the Bus division remained loss-making. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 51-52] In contrast, some end markets showed growth, including Infratech and Foodtech. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 22-23] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 24]

### Principal disclosed risks and responses

VDL identifies several external and operational risks tied to its industrial activities and outlook. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 48-50] These include cost inflation, policy inconsistency, electric vehicle competition from China, supply-chain disruption, cybersecurity, climate risk, foreign exchange (FX) risk, liquidity risk, raw-material risk, energy price risk, market risk, interest rate risk, and credit risk. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 48-50] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 62]

The report also highlights net congestion, energy scarcity, and cybersecurity as important constraints or priorities. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 28-29] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 31] These issues are relevant to VDL’s manufacturing footprint, technology development, and energy-related activities. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 32] [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 69]

Management reports that the transition to new energy forms was slower than hoped in 2025 and that hydrogen projects were “under pressure,” which helps explain the decline in VDL Energy revenue. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 20-21] For Infratech, the 2026 outlook was described as positive but dependent on government funding, indicating exposure to public-sector spending conditions. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 22-23]

The disclosed responses include diversification, automation, pass-through pricing, hedging, and cash-flow monitoring. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, pp. 48-50] The financial-risk policy specifically covers market, currency, interest, credit, liquidity, raw-material, and energy price risk. [vdl-jaarverslag-2025-binnenwerk-nl-web-035.pdf, p. 62]
