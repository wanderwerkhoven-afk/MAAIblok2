## Logbook

> **Fill-in instructions**:
>
> This is the entry point to your logbook. A logbook allows students to record the project's progress.
> Here you should register:
> 1. Schedules and progress summaries.
> 2. Designs and diagrams
> 3. Research documentation
> 4. Feedback you have received from coaches, lecturers, fellow students, etc.
>
> Your coach visits the logbook to get updates on the project's progress. Thus, ensure your logbook is well-organised and readable, has a table of contents, and each component in the log has which team members worked on it. We advise that you divide your logbook per topic and, within each topic, the development per week (if relevant).

> To keep track of your progress, we recommend adding a section at the end of your document called "Coaching sessions", containing a summary of the topics addressed in the meeting, the feedback given by the coach, an action plan, and how the feedback was addressed. 

> Here is an example:
> | Date | Summary of addressed topics | Feedback | Plan | How the feedback was addressed (revisiting) |
>|---|---|---|---|---|
> |Week 2 - dd/mm/yyyy|Activities related to [Paper protoyping (A2)]() as well as [Model metrics (C1)]()| There was only 1 paper prototype. You should expand ideas and validate them with the target group | Brainstorming session | We experimented with other target groups, and the results [can be seen here]() | 

>Feel free to use links pointing to specific document parts, as evidence. It could take some time, maybe more than one week, to address the given feedback. It might be the case that you cannot fill the last column when adding the line. For example, a series of tasks should be performed to address the topic. In such cases, you should revisit all given feedback after these tasks are finished, informing how the feedback was addressed. 

> We advise leveraging collaborative tools available at HvA, such as Gitlab, Teams, and SharePoint. But you are free to choose your preferred tools (e.g. LaTeX, Google Docs, Markdown, Notion, etc.). Independently on the tool used, you should add here a link to the latest PDF version of your logbook, also versioned in this repository.
