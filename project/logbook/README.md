## Logbook

> **Fill-in instructions**:
>
> This is the entry point to your logbook. A logbook allows students to record the project's progress.
> Here you should register:
> 1. Schedules and progress summaries.
> 2. Designs and diagrams
> 3. Research documentation
> 4. Feedback you have received from coaches, lecturers, fellow students, etc.
>
> Your coach visits the logbook to get updates on the project's progress. Thus, ensure your logbook is well-organised and readable, containing a table of contents, and each component in the log has which team members worked on it. 

> You have freedom to choose your preferred editor (e.g. LaTeX, Google Docs, Markdown, Notion, etc) as long as you add here a link to the PDF, also versioned in this repository.

Eventually, you can use parts of the logbook into your report.
